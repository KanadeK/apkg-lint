# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Keep review focused on the work.
- Protect learner and deck-author privacy.
- Accept correction and make repair easy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention,
and publishing another person's private deck or data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or ban contributions or participants when
necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately and
promptly as practical.
